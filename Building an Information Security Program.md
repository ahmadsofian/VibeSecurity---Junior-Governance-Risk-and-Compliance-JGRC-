### An Information Security Program is the comprehensive set of policies, procedures, technologies, and people that protects an organization's information assets.
#### Component of an InfoSec Program
**1. Security Charter & Vision**
* Executive sponsorship and support
* Mission statement for the security program
* Alignment with business objectives
* Budget allocation and resource commitment

**2. Organizational Structure**
CISO / CSO
├── Security Operations (SOC)
├── GRC & Compliance
├── Security Architecture
├── Identity & Access Management
├── Application Security
└── Security Awareness & Training


**3. Policy Framework**
* Information Security Policy (top-level)
* Acceptable Use Policy
* Data Classification Policy
* Access Control Policy
* Incident Response Policy
* Business Continuity Policy
* Vendor Management Policy

**4. Risk Management Program**
* Regular risk assessments
* Risk register maintenance
* Risk treatment plans
* Risk reporting to leadership


**5. Security Controls**
* Technical controls (firewalls, encryption, MFA)
* Administrative controls (policies, training, background checks)
* Physical controls (badges, cameras, locked rooms)


#### Program Maturity Levels
* **Level 1:** Initial — Ad-hoc, reactive security measures
* **Level 2:** Developing — Basic policies exist but inconsistently applied
* **Level 3:** Defined — Documented and standardized processes
* **Level 4:** Managed — Measured and monitored with metrics
* **Level 5:** Optimizing — Continuous improvement with automation


#### Building Program Step by Step
* **Step 1:** Get executive buy-in and sponsorship
* **Step 2:** Conduct a gap assessment against a framework (ISO 27001, NIST)
* **Step 3:** Develop core policies and procedures
* **Step 4:** Implement priority controls based on risk assessment
* **Step 5:** Train employees on security awareness
* **Step 6:** Establish monitoring and incident response capabilities
* **Step 7:** Conduct regular audits and assessments
* **Step 8:** Report metrics to leadership and iterate


#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Percentage of employees completing security training
* Number of open audit findings
* Mean time to detect (MTTD) security incidents
* Mean time to respond (MTTR) to incidents
* Number of policy exceptions granted
* Percentage of systems with up-to-date patches
